Easy PDF Editing: Your New Tool Right in Your Browser!

Do you ever need to quickly change a PDF, but hate downloading special software or uploading your files to websites you don't trust? What if you could fix your PDFs right here, in your web browser, without anyone else seeing your documents?

Good news! We've made a super easy tool that lets you edit PDFs right in your browser. It's fast, private, and simple to use!

What Can This Tool Do?

This handy web tool helps you with common PDF tasks:

  • Upload Your PDF: Just pick any PDF file from your computer to get started.
  • See All Your Pages: You'll see all its pages as small pictures (thumbnails).
  • Delete Pages Easily: Click the 'X' button on any page picture to remove it.
  • Add New Pages: Upload pages from another PDF and insert them anywhere.
  • Move Pages Around (Drag & Drop): Rearrange your PDF pages like puzzle pieces.
  • Download Your Finished PDF: Save your edited PDF right back to your computer.

The best part? Everything happens directly in your browser. Your files stay on your computer, so they're safe and private.

How to Use This PDF Editor

  1. Open the Tool: Save the special HTML code as an .html file (like my-pdf-tool.html) and open it in your browser.
  2. Upload Your First PDF:
    • Click the upload box or drag a PDF file onto it.
    • You’ll see thumbnail previews of all your pages below.
  3. Delete a Page:
    • Hover over a thumbnail and click the 'X' button.
    • Click "OK" when asked to confirm.
  4. Move Pages Around:
    • Click and hold a page thumbnail, drag it to the new position, and release.
  5. Add More Pages:
    • Click the "Add Pages" button and upload another PDF.
    • Choose where to insert it by typing a page number.
    • Click "Confirm Add" to insert pages or "Cancel" to undo.
  6. Download Your Edited PDF:
    • Click the "Download Modified PDF" button when you're finished.
    • The new file will be saved to your computer.
